# Tassula ry - Website Plan
 
## 1. Project Proposal
 
I will create a website for Tassula ry, a volunteer-run animal shelter near Seinäjoki, Finland.
 
The website will help animals find loving homes by introducing animals waiting for adoption and providing information about the adoption process.
 
## 2. Website Goal
 
The main goal is to make it easier for people to find animals looking for homes and contact the shelter about adoption.
 
The website should be simple, clear and easy to use.
 
## 3. Target Visitors
 
The website is intended for:
 
- People interested in adopting cats.
- People interested in adopting dogs.
- People looking to adopt other animals.
- Families searching for a new pet.
- People who want to learn more about Tassula.
 
The website should also be accessible to people who use screen readers.
 
## 4. Website Pages
 
### Home - index.html
H1: Welcome to Tassula ry
 
Introduces the shelter and welcomes visitors.
 
### About Us - about.html
H1: About Tassula ry
 
Explains the organisation, its volunteers and its mission.
 
### Animals - listing.html
H1: Animals Looking for a Home
 
Description about the animal looking for home.
 
### Contact - contact.html
H1: Contact Tassula ry
 
Provides an adoption enquiry form and contact information.
 
## 5. Similar Websites
 
I researched three real animal adoption websites for inspiration:
 
1. Onnentassu - https://www.onnentassu.fi/
2. Pirkanmaan eläinsuojeluyhdistys - https://pesu.org/
3. Kyproskissat ry - https://www.kyproskissat.fi/
 
I will use these websites as inspiration when planning the Tassula Website.

The website will follow the customer's requested colours:
 
- Spruce: #12343b

The website will have simple navigation and clear information.
