Adopting an Animal

At Tassula, we are committed to providing loving homes for animals in need. Our adoption process is designed to ensure a good match between the animal and the new family.

How does Adoption Work?

Step 1: Choose Your Animal

Take your time to browse our available animals and find the perfect companion for your family.

Step 2: Send an Enquiry

Once you've found an animal you're interested in, fill out our adoption enquiry form to learn more about them and the adoption process.

Step 3: Meet Your New Companion

If the initial enquiry is successful, you will be invited to meet your potential new pet and spend some time together to ensure a good match.

Step 4: Welcome your Friend

If everything goes well, you will welcome your new companion into your home!

PS: Before adopting, think about the time, care space and financial responsibilty an animal need. Adopting an animal is a big responsibility!
